01 / ABOUT

I'm Oluwafemi — I go by VICE MAYOR in my work. I'm a Nigerian CS grad who'd rather show you a working repo than tell you a story.

My strongest work so far is in applied computer vision: my final year project is a real-time surveillance system that detects and tracks people and threat objects (knives, guns) from live video, logs events to a database, and triggers alerts — trained on a custom Roboflow dataset, running YOLOv11n and ByteTrack under a Flask dashboard.

Alongside that, I design and ship full-stack web builds — landing pages, business sites, and product concepts — usually static-first and deployed on Netlify. I'm currently going deeper into offensive security and penetration testing, which is the direction I want my next chapter to move toward.
