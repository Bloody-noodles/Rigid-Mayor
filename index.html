01 / About

I’m Oluwafemi Waya, the builder behind Rigid Mayor. I turn business needs and rough ideas into useful digital experiences: websites that explain an offer clearly, enquiry paths that make it easier to contact a business, and practical tools that reduce friction.

My primary focus is business websites and customer enquiry systems. I also build AI-enabled tools and software prototypes, with computer vision as a deeper technical specialty. I use AI-assisted workflows where they help, while checking implementation and being clear about what has actually been tested.

The goal is not technology for its own sake. It is to make the next step clearer for customers and the work more effective for the business. Explore this portfolio or visit my specialist web portfolio.
